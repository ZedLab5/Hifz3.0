# Noor (نور) - Modern Islamic Spiritual Companion

Noor is an ultra-modern, beautifully designed Islamic companion application built natively for Android using Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3.

## Features

- **Daily Dashboard**: Real-time prayer times countdown, daily Ayat & Hadith revelation, spiritual streak & habit tracking, and customizable quick-access cards.
- **Noble Quran Reader & Audio Player**: Complete 114 Surahs with Arabic typography, transliteration, English translations, Mushaf flow view, bookmarking, search, and reciter audio playback.
- **Curated Mood & Spiritual Reflections**: Mood-based reflection picker pairing emotional states (Anxious, Grateful, Tired, Hopeful, Lost, Peaceful) with authentic Quranic verses, prophetic wisdom, and actionable reflection.
- **Daily Duas & Azkar**: Authentic morning, evening, and situational supplications categorized with progress counters, translation, transliteration, and audio support.
- **Digital Tasbih**: Customizable dhikr counter with target goals, haptic feedback, presets, and session history.
- **Salat Tracker & Prayer Timetable**: Precise prayer times calculation with multiple worldwide authorities, Hanafi/Standard Asr juristic calculation, manual minute adjustments, adhan audio previews, and daily prayer completion tracking.
- **Qibla Compass**: Sensor-based real-time directional compass pointing towards the Kaaba in Makkah with sensor accuracy calibration.
- **Quran Khatma Tracker**: Structured 30-day and custom Khatma completion plans with reading targets and progress tracking.
- **Home Screen Widget**: Glance-based Android widget for live prayer times and upcoming prayer countdown directly on the home screen.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Kotlin & Jetpack Compose
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Coroutines & StateFlow
- **Design System**: Material Design 3 (M3) with dynamic light, dark, and warm palettes
- **Local Persistence**: Room Database
- **Image Loading**: Coil
- **Widgets**: Jetpack Glance Material 3
